# dsh-tool-realestatepro

Real Estate Pro Investment & Property Management Agent for DeepSeek Harness.

Advanced property valuation (multi-model AVM), investment return analysis with leverage IRR, AI tenant screening, property management orchestration, market trend compass, financing optimizer, location risk assessor, and portfolio builder.

## Tools

1. **property_valuation** - Automated Valuation Model (AVM) combining comparable sales, hedonic price modeling, income approach, and cost approach with confidence intervals
2. **investment_analyzer** - Investment return analysis with cash-on-cash, Cap Rate, leveraged IRR, exit sensitivity, and holding period optimization
3. **tenant_screening** - AI-powered tenant screening with credit verification, income validation, risk scoring, and optimal tenant selection
4. **property_management** - Full property management orchestration including lease management, rent collection, maintenance, compliance, and owner reporting
5. **market_compass** - Market trend analysis with price movements, inventory cycles, days on market, interest rate impact, and policy risk
6. **financing_optimizer** - Financing optimization with refinance analysis, LTV/DCR metrics, multi-scenario comparison, and capital structure advice
7. **risk_locator** - Location risk assessment covering crime, schools, disaster hazards, environmental factors, future planning impact, and composite scoring
8. **portfolio_builder** - Portfolio construction with diversification analysis, correlation matrix, rebalancing, REIT comparison, and after-tax return optimization

## Theme

Deep blue real estate dashboard aesthetic with valuation gauges and market heat maps.
