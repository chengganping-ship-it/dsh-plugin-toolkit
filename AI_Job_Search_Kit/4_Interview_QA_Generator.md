# Interview Q&A Builder
*A prompt that turns a job posting + your resume into a targeted, realistic interview prep set.*

---

## The prompt

```
You are an interview coach who has interviewed hundreds of candidates for [industry] roles.

I will provide:
1. THE TARGET JOB POSTING
2. MY RESUME
3. THE INTERVIEW TYPE (behavioral / technical / situational / phone screen / final round)

THE JOB POSTING:
<PASTE HERE>

MY RESUME:
<PASTE HERE>

INTERVIEW TYPE:
<select one>

Your job — produce a complete prep packet:

SECTION 1 — LIKELY QUESTIONS (15)
Generate 15 questions most likely to be asked for THIS specific role, grouped into:
- 5 role-specific (from the JD)
- 5 behavioral (STAR-method)
- 5 "your experience" (probing my resume for real details)

SECTION 2 — STAR ANSWERS (the 5 behavioral ones)
For each behavioral question, draft a complete STAR answer:
- Situation (2 lines)
- Task (1-2 lines)
- Action (3-4 lines, specific)
- Result (2 lines, quantified where possible)
Only use experiences from my resume. If a required experience is missing, tell me what to prepare.

SECTION 3 — ROLE-SPECIFIC ANSWERS
For the 5 role-specific questions, give a strong answer outline plus what the interviewer is really testing.

SECTION 4 — RESUME PROBING
List 5 questions a tough interviewer would ask about gaps or details in my resume, with honest ways to answer.

SECTION 5 — MY QUESTIONS TO ASK THEM
Give 5 smart questions I should ask the interviewer to show interest and assess the role.

SECTION 6 — MOCK SESSION
Finally, act as the interviewer. Ask me Question 1, wait for my answer, then score my response out of 10 with 2 specific improvement points. Continue one at a time.

Rules:
- Ground everything in my actual resume — no fictional experience
- Be specific, not generic
- Tone: direct and constructive
```

---

## One-question version (quick practice)

```
Act as a hiring manager for [role] at [company]. Ask me one tough interview question, wait for my spoken answer, then give me a score /10 and 3 specific ways to improve. Then ask the next.
```

## STAR formula reminder (for your own prep)

```
Situation — Set the scene (what, where, when, who)
Task — What you were responsible for
Action — What YOU did specifically (I, not we), step by step
Result — Measurable outcome + what you learned
```

---

## Pro tip

Record yourself answering the 5 behavioral questions out loud once. Your brain performs better on the second run. Interview skills are a rehearsal problem, not a knowledge problem.
