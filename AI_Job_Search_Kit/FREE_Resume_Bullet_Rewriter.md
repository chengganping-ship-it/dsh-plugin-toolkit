# FREE AI Resume Bullet Rewriter
*A free sample — one focused prompt that rewrites weak resume bullets into strong, ATS-friendly, result-driven ones.*

---

## Why this free sample exists

Most resumes describe *responsibilities*, not *results*. This single prompt fixes the most common resume problem: turning "I did X" into "I did X and it produced Y."

If this helps you, the full **AI Job Search Kit** extends the same method to cover letters, LinkedIn, interviews, and application tracking.

---

## Paste this into ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini

```
You are a professional resume writer who turns weak bullet points into strong, quantified, ATS-friendly ones.

I'll give you a list of resume bullet points and the type of role I'm applying for.

MY BULLET POINTS:
<PASTE YOUR BULLETS HERE>

TARGET ROLE:
<e.g. Digital Marketing Specialist>

For EACH bullet, rewrite it using this formula:
[Action verb] + [what you did] + [how] + [result]

Rules:
1. Start with a strong action verb (Led, Built, Reduced, Increased, Launched, Negotiated, Automated).
2. If the original has no number, write [add your number] in its place — DO NOT invent a metric.
3. Keep each bullet under 2 lines.
4. Never invent experience, tools, companies, or achievements that aren't in my original bullet.
5. Make it specific and result-oriented, not vague.

Output format: for each original bullet, show:
- ❌ Original:
- ✅ Rewritten:
- 💡 Why it works (1 short line)

If a bullet is already strong, say so and suggest only a minor polish.
```

---

## Example — what good looks like

**❌ Original:** "Responsible for managing social media accounts."

**✅ Rewritten:** "Managed 3 social media accounts and grew engagement 42% in 6 months through weekly content testing. [add your real numbers]"

**💡 Why it works:** Leads with an action verb, adds a quantified result, and the `[add your number]` forces you to fill in your real metric instead of letting AI invent one.
