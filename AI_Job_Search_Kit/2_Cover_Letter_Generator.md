# Cover Letter Generator
*A prompt that produces tailored, non-templated cover letters that match the specific job — not a generic form letter.*

---

## The prompt

```
You are a persuasive yet honest cover letter writer and career strategist.

I will provide:
1. THE JOB POSTING
2. MY RESUME / RELEVANT EXPERIENCE
3. THE COMPANY (name + one line about what they do)

THE JOB POSTING:
<PASTE HERE>

MY RELEVANT EXPERIENCE:
<PASTE HERE>

THE COMPANY:
<name + one line>

Write a cover letter that is:
- 3 short paragraphs (under 250 words total)
- Opening: 1-2 lines that connect MY specific experience to THEIR specific problem or mission (never a generic "I am excited to apply")
- Middle: ONE specific example of a result I delivered that maps to a requirement in the posting
- Close: a clear, low-pressure call to action and confidence statement

Rules:
- Never use these phrases: "I am writing to apply for", "I believe I am a great fit", "Enclosed please find", "I look forward to hearing from you soon"
- Mention the company name and one detail from their posting or website to prove I read it
- Sound like a confident human, not a template
- No invented facts. Use [brackets] for anything uncertain.
- Match the tone of the posting (formal vs casual)

Output ONLY the cover letter. Then add a short "Why this works" note (3 bullet points).
```

---

## Variations

**Short / email version (best for LinkedIn or application forms):**
```
Rewrite the same letter as a 4-5 sentence email version. Subject line included. No "Dear Sir/Madam".
```

**Cold outreach version (no job posted yet):**
```
Rewrite as a cold outreach note to a hiring manager whose company does [X]. Goal: get a 15-min call, not a job offer. Keep under 120 words.
```

**Career changer:**
```
Rewrite focusing on my transferable skills and genuine motivation to switch from [old] to [new]. Explain briefly WHY the switch (honest reason). Keep it positive, not defensive.
```

---

## Pro tip

Always personalize the company detail. Spend 2 minutes on their website and grab ONE specific thing (a product, a blog post, a recent hire). Drop that into the `<THE COMPANY>` field. This single move separates your letter from 90% of applicants.
