# LinkedIn Profile Optimization Template
*Headline, About, and Experience rewrite prompts — optimized for recruiter search on LinkedIn.*

---

## Prompt 1 — Headline (the line under your name)

```
You are a LinkedIn profile optimization expert for [industry].

My current title: [your current role]
My target role: [target role]
My top 3 skills: [skill 1], [skill 2], [skill 3]
One quantified achievement: [e.g. "grew revenue 40%"]

Write 5 LinkedIn headline options, each under 120 characters, that:
- Include my target role or an aspirational-but-honest title
- Include at least one high-value keyword recruiters search
- Optionally include one result or differentiator
- Do NOT use buzzwords like "guru", "ninja", "results-driven", "passionate"
Rank them best-first. Explain in one line why #1 is best.
```

## Prompt 2 — About section (the "About" block)

```
You are a LinkedIn profile writer.

Write my About section in 5 short paragraphs or punchy lines:
1. Who I am (title + what I do) — 1 line
2. The problem I help solve — 1-2 lines
3. My proof (1-2 quantified achievements) — 1-2 lines
4. Who I've worked with / my niche — 1 line
5. A soft call to action + what I'm open to (new role, consulting, networking)

My details:
<PASTE YOUR DETAILS HERE>

Rules:
- Written in first person
- Under 1500 characters total (LinkedIn limit is ~2600, shorter is better)
- Keywords naturally woven in, not stuffed
- Honest, no fabrication
- Tone: confident and specific
```

## Prompt 3 — Experience bullets (aligned to resume)

```
Convert these resume bullets into LinkedIn-optimized versions (they read differently than resumes):

<PASTE RESUME BULLETS>

For each:
- Keep it to 1-2 lines
- Use the same action-verb + result formula
- Add a subtle human touch (context, challenge overcome) that a resume wouldn't include
- Keep keywords intact
Output as bullet list under each role.
```

---

## LinkedIn-specific checklist

- [ ] Custom profile URL (e.g. linkedin.com/in/yourname)
- [ ] Professional headshot (not a crop of a group photo)
- [ ] Banner image with your value proposition
- [ ] Headline: 120 chars, keyword-rich, no "seeking opportunities" (recruiters filter it)
- [ ] About: 3-5 short paragraphs, first person, specific results
- [ ] "Open to Work" tag set to your target roles
- [ ] Featured section: pin your best project/post
- [ ] At least 3 skills verified (take skill quizzes)
- [ ] Recommendations: request 2 from managers/colleagues

## Pro tip

Set your LinkedIn headline to your TARGET role, not your current one, if you're job hunting — recruiters search by target title. Keep it honest: "Marketing Analyst | Data-driven growth | ex-[company]" is fine and searches well.
