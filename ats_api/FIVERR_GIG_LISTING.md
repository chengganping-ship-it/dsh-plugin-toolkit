## Description (SEO-Optimized)

**Landing an interview starts with passing the ATS filter.**

Most companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to screen resumes before a human ever sees them. If your resume doesn't contain the right keywords in the right format, it gets filtered out — no matter how qualified you are.

**Our ATS Keyword Gap Analysis gives you a precise, actionable roadmap to optimize your resume for any job posting.**

### ✅ What You Get:
- **ATS Compatibility Score** (0-100) — know exactly where you stand
- **Missing Keywords Detection** — pinpoint exactly what's absent from your resume
- **Domain Breakdown** — see gaps by category (Technical, Soft Skills, Education, Experience)
- **Actionable Rewrite Suggestions** — specific phrases and keywords to add
- **Before/After Comparison** — understand exactly what changed

### 📋 How It Works:
1. **Send us** your resume (text or paste) and the target job description
2. **Our system analyzes** both documents against 50+ ATS criteria
3. **You receive** a detailed report with an optimization roadmap
4. **Apply with confidence** — your resume is now ATS-optimized

### 🔄 Revisions:
- Standard & Premium include up to 2 revision rounds
- Submit your updated resume, we'll re-analyze
