## 5. FAQ (消除疑虑)

### Q1: What if the generated motion doesn't fit my character model?

**A:** This is the most common concern, and it's exactly why I include **professional retargeting** in Standard and Premium packages. The AI generates motion on a standard humanoid skeleton (SOMA-RP, 30 joints). I then use industry-standard retargeting tools to map that motion onto YOUR specific character — whether it's a VRM anime model, a Mixamo rig, a MetaHuman, or a custom skeleton. The result is motion that respects your character's proportions and joint limits. If something looks off, the included revision rounds fix it.

---

---

### Q3: AI-generated motion looks robotic. How do you ensure quality?

**A:** You're right to be skeptical — raw AI output often has foot sliding, unnatural easing, or joint popping. That's why my service is **NOT just "run AI and send you the file."** Here's my quality pipeline:

1. **AI Generation** → Raw skeletal motion (fast but imperfect)
2. **Automated Cleanup** → Foot locking, joint limit enforcement, velocity clamping
3. **Manual Polish** → I (a professional animator) review frame-by-frame, add easing curves, fix intersections, ensure weight and momentum feel right
4. **Validation** → Playblast in engine, check for jitter, verify loop points

The result: **AI speed + human quality.** Every animation is watched in real-time before delivery. If it doesn't look natural, it doesn't ship.

---

### Q4: What engines and software are compatible?

**A:** The FBX/BVH files I deliver work with:

If your pipeline uses something else, message me — I can likely export to your format.
