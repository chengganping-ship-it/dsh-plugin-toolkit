# AI Era Time Capsule Intelligence

> Each snapshot is testimony that cannot be recreated.

---

## What This Is

Not an opinion column. Not a paid research feed.

This is a date-anchored data snapshot archive. Each report asks: **"On this day, what did we know about AI infrastructure's world, what could we prove, and what might be falsified later?"**

We capture data that cannot be precisely recreated once the moment passes — verbal declarations on earnings calls, pricing pages not yet archived, policy texts not yet scrubbed, first-source figures not yet diluted by secondary citation.

---

## Why Not "Just Search It"

Search engines give you "now." But "vanished data" has no index.

- OpenAI's pricing page has changed 7 times. Only Wayback Machine and our snapshot preserve the 2025-08-08 16:30 Beijing time prices.
- The precise wording of De Minimis termination in the Federal Register will be overwritten by simplified narrative accounts within 6 months.
- The US Commerce Bureau's "use is violation" guidance on Huawei Ascend was released Friday afternoon, paraphrased heavily over the weekend, and had 3 competing interpretations by Monday.

We record the originals.

## Data Provenance Standard

We self-grade:

- **Tier 1 [Verified]** — Primary official source, directly fetched
- **Tier 2 [Inferred]** — Calculated from ≥2 verified points
- **Tier 3 [Unverified]** — Single source, explicitly labeled

Every figure includes source URL, access date, replication method. See [data-provenance-tier-spec.md](./data-provenance-tier-spec.md).

## Methodology & Limits

- **Every report contains a "Counters: Where We May Be Wrong" section.** Not humility — epistemic hygiene.
- **Every report references failure cases.** We collect historical projects (IBM Watson MD Anderson, US transformer capacity pledge, Shenzhen seller SEA exodus) as reference frames.
- **We use AI as tool, not author.** Data verification, source tracing, and comparative analysis rely heavily on LLM assistance, but human judgment makes the final call. A [Verified] label means we visited the original URL and content matched.
